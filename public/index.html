          Supported formats & how we count
          

            

              Upload Word (.docx), PowerPoint (.pptx), selectable-text PDF,
              Excel (.xlsx / .xls), OpenDocument, RTF, HTML or readable Unicode
              text. Maximum file size: 3 MB; maximum extracted text: 100,000
              characters; PDFs: 200 pages. Scanned PDFs and images need OCR
              first. Export legacy .doc / .ppt documents before uploading.
            

            

              Words are lowercased. Punctuation separates tokens: “don't”
              becomes “don” and “t”, while “12.5” becomes “12” and “5”. Unicode
              letters and numbers remain; no stopwords are removed. Character
              counts include spaces and punctuation. Spreadsheet formulas are
              not evaluated; stored or cached values are read when available.
            

            

              Your document is sent to the analysis service for processing
              without writing it to disk. Analysis does not require an account.
